Example memories. In the app, these are yours.

    Save it now. Find it later.

    Most memories need only two things: what it is, and where it is. Everything else is optional.

    Write it

    Type the item and the place. Two fields, one tap.

    Say it

    “I put the spare key in the inside pocket of my black coat.”

    The app fills in the item and the place for you to check.

    Snap it

    Add a photo when words aren't enough. Location data is stripped from it first.

    Calm, fast and private

    Forgiving search

    Search the item, the place or your note. Capitals and accents don't matter: cekmece finds Çekmece.

    Works offline

    Everything is saved on your phone first. It syncs to your account when you're online, so a new phone doesn't mean starting over.

    Only you can see it

    Database rules allow only your account to read your memories. They are never sold or shared with advertisers.

    Delete any time

    Remove a single memory, or delete your whole account and every photo, from inside the app.
